FEATURED BUILD

The official app for the University Blockchain Conference

Students fly into UT Austin for two days of overlapping sessions on a campus they've never seen. Everything used to live in signs and emails. I built one app for all of it in Claude Code, deployed it from GitHub, and it ran the whole weekend: schedules that find the gaps in your day, profiles for every speaker, room-to-room walking directions, and Proof of Brew, which set up blind coffee chats between students from different schools.
